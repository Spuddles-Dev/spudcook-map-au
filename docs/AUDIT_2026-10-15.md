## Freshness limits

15 October. Matching public candidates prove completion of that export, not that
the live backend has no later changes. The host publication service was retried
after its hourly preflight reported pending publication. Materialization was
still active while additional backend dirty revisions arrived. Later runs may
publish newer data; rerun the audit against a refreshed checkout to measure it.

Historical `data/` exports remain historical. Price observation times and food
verification remain independent of archive publication timestamps. No missing
mapping or verification was fabricated to increase coverage.
